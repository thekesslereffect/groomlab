GroomLab is in early access, and it changes quickly. This page lists the improvements that change
how you work, newest first. It will keep growing as the system is fleshed out. Something missing,
or something you would like to see? Write to
[support@getgroomlab.com](mailto:support@getgroomlab.com).

**Check for Updates** in the GroomLab menu (the app: **Help > Check for Updates**) gets the latest
version. While GroomLab is in early access, a node's params can still change between updates (a
param renamed, or two merged into one); the entries below say when one does and what to set. Once
it leaves early access, saved grooms will keep their look across updates.

## 0.37.0 (October 30, 2026)

- **A cleaner, more modern UI** in Maya, the app and Blender. Charcoal is now the default theme
  everywhere (a blue accent, the Inter font, underlined tabs, quiet uppercase section headers), and
  the Graph Editor's nodes have filled headers with their status on the card: the cook time (yellow
  from 10 ms), a warning triangle, bypassed nodes faded with dashed wires. Pick a theme in
  **GroomLab > Theme** (the app: **View > Theme**); a theme you picked before stays.
- **Sliders you can type into.** Every number is one field with its name inside: drag to scrub
  (Shift: finer, Ctrl: coarser), or click and type a value or a little arithmetic (`0.5*2`, `1/3`);
  Enter or Tab keeps it, Esc puts it back, the arrow keys nudge it. A typed value may go past the
  slider's range.
- **The Groom Tools, reorganized.** The header, tabs and tool tiles stay put; the picked tool's
  settings sit right under the tiles, always in the same order: **Brush** (radius, strength,
  falloff and its toggles), the tool's own settings (Paint's mode, value and Fill; Place's new
  guides and tube; the Shape brush's profile editor; Erase, Length Keys, Flow, Parts), then
  **Applies to** (Acts on, Mirror). A **?** on the card shows the tool's full hint and the
  gestures. Under it, the tab's list: guide groups (click one to pick it; Isolate is the eye in its
  header), maps, correctives or photos, with the picked one's own settings under the list.
- **Moved:** Selected only and Front only are no longer in the panel (brushes keep their defaults);
  the Output buttons are in the GroomLab menu (the app: **Render** and **Export** at the right of the
  menu bar); While sculpting is in the groom's settings; Fill Tubes and Tube Blend are the
  Interpolate node's settings.
- **Length Keys replace Length Paths.** Click the scalp to drop a key (it changes nothing yet),
  right-drag a key (Maya: middle-drag) to scale the guide lengths around it; the keys' scales blend
  over the scalp along the skin and ease back past their spacing. **Apply** bakes the lengths (one
  undo step) and the keys stay, back at x1. In Maya, the app and Blender.

## 0.36.0 (October 30, 2026)

- **Length Paths.** A new tool (Trim) that sets guide lengths from paths of keys you lay on the
  scalp: click to lay keys, drag them to move them, right-drag a key up or down to set its length
  (Maya: middle-drag), Shift+drag to size its reach. Lengths blend between keys and fade out at
  each key's reach; several paths (one per leg) blend too. Guides preview until **Apply** (one undo
  step); the paths are saved with the groom to edit and apply again. Symmetry mirrors the paths,
  topology symmetry to each key's partner spot. In Maya, the app and Blender. (Replaced by
  [Length Keys](/docs/guides-and-brushes#length-keys) in the next release.)
- **Shape brush: Shift+drag smooths again.** It evens out the tube channel it paints (width,
  flatness or twist) along the guides; the other guide brushes still select with Shift.

## 0.35.2 (October 28, 2026)

- **Fixes:** opening a scene saved by a newer GroomLab no longer fills the Script Editor with a
  Graph Editor traceback: the Graph Editor says the graph needs a newer GroomLab and leaves it as it
  is (update GroomLab to edit it). A scene saved while GroomLab was loaded from a renamed copy of the
  plugin (a development build) now opens with its grooms when you double-click it.

## 0.35.1 (October 28, 2026)

- **Changed: Shift selects, Ctrl flips.** With a guide brush, **Shift+drag** paints guides into the
  selection and **Shift+click** adds the guides in the ring (on bare scalp: deselects and unfreezes
  all); this was Ctrl. **Ctrl** now does every brush's opposite: Length shortens, Lift lays down,
  Twist, Curl, Attract, Direction, Noise and Shape go the other way, Cut grows, painting goes
  toward 0. The brush lasso is gone (the **Select** tool keeps its own). Painting maps or Freeze,
  Shift+drag still smooths; smooth guides with the **Smooth** brush.
- **Blender: updates from inside Blender.** **GroomLab > Help > Check for Updates...** shows the
  newest version with what's new, and **Update** installs it in place (restart Blender to finish).
  Once a day GroomLab checks at startup and says so in the Groom Tools. Both need Blender's
  **Allow Online Access**. From this version on there's no need to download GroomLab from the site
  to update it.

## 0.35.0 (October 28, 2026)

- **Brush alphas.** The Paint brush on a vertex or texture map can stamp an alpha: a grayscale
  image inside the brush circle, so a stroke leaves speckles, spots, noise or streaks instead of a
  round spot. Pick one from the **Alpha** thumbnail (eight ship with GroomLab), or **Add Alpha...**
  your own: they go to `Documents/GroomLab/alphas`, which Maya, Blender and the app share. Set its
  **Rotation** (fixed, following the stroke, or random), **Spacing** and **Invert**; the alpha
  multiplies the brush's falloff, and symmetry mirrors it. See
  [Brush alphas](/docs/painting-maps#brush-alphas).
- **Segments per group and per guide.** Each guide group keeps its own segment count, which its
  new guides take. The Segments slider rebuilds the selected guides, else the picked group, else
  every guide. Guides you resample on their own keep their count when the group's changes; the
  group's right-click **Reset Segments** brings them back in line. Frozen guides are never
  resampled.
- **Changed: no Display Segments.** The guides' dots sit on their own points (still set by
  **Guide Points**: all, roots and tips, or off).
- **Blender: Create Groom without a preset.** A new groom starts from the default graph fitted to
  the mesh, as in Maya and the app; pick a look later from the Presets menu.
- **Fixes:** the Groom Tools and the Graph Editor docked back beside Maya's Toolbox can be resized
  again.

## 0.34.0 (October 28, 2026)

- **Interpolate: strands between parted guides keep their length.** A new `blend` param:
  `directions` (the default for new Interpolate nodes) builds each strand segment by segment
  along its guides' directions, so hair between guides combed apart turns smoothly from one side
  to the other instead of being pulled in. `offsets` is the old average. One **smoothness**
  slider now sets how softly the guides blend into one another, and Keep Clearance checks the
  skin at every point, so strands no longer dip in between two points.
- **Guide weight.** Interpolate's `guideWeight` sets how strongly each guide pulls the strands
  around it against its neighbours. Paint it: **Paint > + New Map > Guide weight**.
- **Cards along the guides, flat feather cards.** Cards can follow a Guides node (`cardsFrom`
  guides, or draw a link into its new `guides` input). New Cards nodes flatten feather cards to
  their vane (Feather Flatten 1) and no longer push feathers apart (Feather Separation 0).
- **Clump takes a Guides node's curves.** Linking a Guides node into Clump's centers sets
  `inputCurves`.
- **Collide: one softness.** `softness` is now a length along the strand: hair starts curving
  that far before it meets a collider and slides along it, resting at the offset. It replaces
  `softness` and `smoothing`; the old softness also floated hair off the surface. Set `softness`
  again on a Collide that used either.
- **Even roots that hold at any scale.** Scatter spreads its roots with a smooth relaxation, so a
  scalp in other units (cm or m) grows the same hair, and an edit moves roots only near it. Its
  `randomness` stops at 1.
- **Fixes:** Wind and Part no longer flip a segment pointing nearly against them (hair combed
  across a part). Instance's leaves no longer jump a quarter turn as their strands move, and
  `spinRandom` turns them either way. Mirror flips feather vanes. A Scatter density from an
  expression above 1 is no longer cut off. Clump locks no longer cross their strands, and a
  negative Clump pushes strands out in order. Bend leaves a strand standing straight off the skin
  as it leaves one a hair off it, and Displace reads its profile along the strand's length, not
  its points. Guides level -1 takes each family's deepest groups. A Ctrl+Shift lasso drawn off the
  guides deselects and unfreezes them all.
- **Simpler nodes.** Rarely used params moved under Advanced, and a few changed. Saved nodes keep
  their old defaults (Upgrade in the Graph Editor moves them to the new ones). Set these again if
  you used them:
  - Renamed: Length `trimMax` is `randomness`, Clump `flatten` is `flatAlong` (`flatDirection`),
    Bands `randomness` is `melaninRandom`, Tangle `sameClump` is `clumpChannel`, Merge `match`
    is `strength`.
  - Removed: Length `trimMin`, Gravity `directionColorSet` (use an expression in `direction`),
    Interpolate `reach`, `power` and `smoothGuides` (use `smoothness`), Clump `copyNeighbors`.
  - New defaults for new nodes: Noise and FieldNoise one octave, Flyaway `fraction` 0.01,
    Direction along `direction` (no line at the scalp's UV seams), FeatherColor `downStrength`
    1, Split `select` 1, Blend `keepLength` on (each strand keeps its length between the two
    looks).
  - Split moved to Utility. The Cinematic hair preset is rebuilt.

## 0.33.0 (October 27, 2026)

- **Faster grooming on dense grooms.** Combing guides on a 181,000-strand wolf takes about 14 ms
  a dab (it took 44), and opening it evaluates in under a second (it took three). Every look node
  now runs one set of math on the CPU and in the GPU preview, so the two always agree. Instances
  that a dab leaves alone are no longer rebuilt, and combing no longer nudges guide roots.
- **Faster previews of Cull, Split and Mirror.** Dragging a param before them stays in the GPU
  preview.
- **Braid: a paths output, looser box braids.** Braid's new `paths` output hands on the paths it
  braids along as guides for the nodes after it. Box braids weave at 0.3 crossings, and a braid
  stays round however few points its strands bring.
- **Changed: Curl adds its points first.** Where a strand has too few points for its curls, Curl
  now adds them along the strand's smooth curve before it curls. Tight curls on sparse strands can
  look slightly different.
- **Removed: the Tie node.** Make a ponytail with **Braid** instead: draw the path from the
  ponytail's base and give it a large `region` (0.45, say), with `alongScalp` on so hair from
  afar sweeps over the scalp into it. A groom saved with a Tie node no longer loads: delete its Tie
  nodes in 0.32 before you update.

## 0.32.1 (October 25, 2026)

- **Changed: Ctrl selects guides.** With a guide brush (Comb, Smooth, Lift, Length, Cut...),
  **Ctrl+click** selects every guide inside the brush ring (no more aiming right at one), and
  **Ctrl+drag** paints guides into the selection; **Ctrl+Shift+drag** draws a lasso around the
  guides you want. Every guide not selected freezes, one undo step; Ctrl+click where the ring holds
  no guide deselects and unfreezes all. Ctrl no longer reverses a guide brush: use a negative
  strength (Lift, Length, Twist, Shape). Painting maps and Freeze keep Ctrl as the opposite
  (Ctrl+Paint on density removes hair, Ctrl+Freeze unfreezes).
- **Fixed: feather cards shearing at the tip.** On tightly curled feathers the barbs near the tip
  could flip a card's side and shear it. Cards now lie flat in the vane to the tip.

## 0.32.0 (October 25, 2026)

- **Select guides without leaving the brush.** **Ctrl+Shift+drag** paints guides into the
  selection with the brush ring, live, and when you let go every guide not selected freezes, so
  the brush works on just your selection (one undo step). **Ctrl+Shift+click** adds one guide;
  **Ctrl+Shift+click** off a guide deselects and unfreezes all. Selection only takes guides you
  can see: nothing behind the head.
- **Changed: Shift+click no longer solos.** Shift is smoothing alone (Shift+drag); selecting is
  Ctrl+Shift in every host, as above.
- **Compounds preview on the GPU.** A compound is a grouping, as in Unreal and Blender: its nodes
  now run as if they sat in the graph, so dragging a compound's parameters (Soft Frizz,
  Windswept Ends, Grey Streaks, your own) previews on the GPU, and so do the nodes above it.
  Saved grooms and the Graph Editor are unchanged.
- **Drags stay on the GPU past a Subdivide.** With Subdivide at the end of the graph, a drag
  previews unsmoothed on the GPU and the release smooths again (before, every drag fell back to
  the CPU).

## 0.31.2 (October 25, 2026)

- **Fixed: stair-stepped strands in card bakes.** On wide cards that bend, baked strands ran in
  steps (and some pieces landed in the wrong place). Strands now follow the card smoothly.
- **Fixed: hair cards with wide tips.** A few cards flared out at the tip for the handful of
  longer strands running past the rest, or for one strand drifting off its clump. Cards now keep
  to their lock.
- **Flyaways get cards of their own.** Strands a Flyaway node pushes out no longer widen their
  clump's card: each gets a thin one-strand card, first in line for **Flyaway Cards** (the
  longest first).

## 0.31.1 (October 24, 2026)

- **Fixed: Separation could shear cards.** In a dense groom a few cards were bent at the tip or
  shoved far from their strands (sometimes more than a feather's length). Separation now never
  moves a card more than its neighbours ask, nor more than half its width, and the card stays
  smooth.
- **Fixed: a new guide group is highlighted in the Groom Tools list** right away, as it already
  was in the Group choice above it.

## 0.31.0 (October 24, 2026)

- **Cards that don't pass through each other.** The Cards node's new **Separation** section pushes
  cards that cross or nearly touch apart along their normals, so overlapping feathers lie on each
  other instead of clipping. **Feather Separation** is on by default (0.05 of a card's width);
  **Separation** does the same for hair cards (off by default). Roots stay put, each card bends
  smoothly as a whole, and only the mesh moves: baked maps stay valid. (Requested by
  Omar Hesham.)
- **Small numbers show.** A value under 1 now keeps two significant digits in Maya and the app:
  a feather preset's Scatter density on a centimeter-sized head reads 0.035 rather than 0 (it
  always scattered correctly).
- **App:** Alt+right-drag zooms the way Maya does (drag right to zoom in), and the app has the
  GroomLab icon.
- **Blender:** choosing one of Blender's tools (the toolbar, W) now leaves the GroomLab brush, so
  objects can be selected again without pressing Esc first. Add Sample Mesh frames the view on the
  sample, which comes in at its real size (a head is about 35 cm).

## 0.30.0 (October 24, 2026)

- **GroomLab files in Maya's File Path Editor.** Every file a groom uses shows under **GroomLab**:
  its texture maps, the images and Alembic files its graph reads, its guide cache, Simulate bakes,
  card bake and the groom file it renders from. Repath them there, or with Replace String, and the
  groom follows, its paths kept relative to the scene. A scene opened from another folder or
  machine keeps its grooms' paths; the entries just show where the files are now. (Requested by
  Omar Hesham.)
- **For pipelines.** Farm submitters, publish and archive scripts can collect a groom's files with
  `groomlab.file_dependencies(groom)` and repoint one with `groomlab.repath_file`. Maya's own
  Archive Scene only sees Maya's node types, so use these (or Collect Files) for GroomLab's files.
- **Collect Files.** GroomLab > Utilities > **Collect Files** copies every file the grooms use from
  outside the scene's folder into its groomlab folder and points the grooms at the copies, so the
  scene's folder holds everything for Archive Scene, a zip or another machine. One undo step.
- **Fixed: Save As left some files behind.** Saving a scene under a new name or folder copied its
  texture maps, card bakes and caches along, but not Simulate bakes or the images and Alembic files
  the graph reads (HairColor's and FeatherColor's images, ImportCurves' file): they went missing in
  the new scene. Now they come along too.

## 0.29.0 (October 24, 2026)

- **Pins in the Graph Editor.** Tidy crossing wires the way Unreal's Blueprints do: double-click a
  link to put a pin on it, then drag the pin to route the link. Drop a pin on another pin of the
  same output and they join, so links from one output run through one pin as a bundle. Drag from a
  pin's right side to an input for another link of its signal; drop a link from an output on a pin
  to feed every link through it from there. Select a pin and press **Delete** to remove it (the
  link stays). In Maya and the app; Blender shows pins as its own reroute nodes. Pins are saved
  with the groom and travel between all three.
- **Snap.** A **Snap** toggle on the Graph Editor's top bar puts the nodes and pins you drag on
  the grid. It's remembered between sessions.
- **Cut links with Alt+Shift+drag.** As in Maya's Hypershade: every link the line crosses turns
  red, and letting go disconnects them as one edit. (From Omar Hesham's pull request.)
- **Undo an edit that left the graph unfinished.** Cutting or deleting a link that leaves the
  output unconnected isn't applied to the groom until it is wired again; Ctrl+Z now puts the graph
  back, instead of undoing the step before it.
- **Normal map for game cards.** Every card bake and export writes a tangent-space `_Normal` map
  beside the others: DirectX's green in Unreal's TGAs, OpenGL's for Unity's PNGs and glTF's normal
  texture. `_Tangent` keeps its name (Unreal's own slot for the strand direction); use `_Normal` in
  a card material that doesn't use Unreal's Hair shading model. (From Omar Hesham's pull request.)
- **Wireframe on shaded for cards in Maya.** The groom's cards view now draws the cards' edges
  with Shading > Wireframe on Shaded, in Maya's wireframe color (lead or active when selected), and
  Wireframe mode shows the edges alone, like any mesh.
- **Clump: Strand Strength Random.** In Clump's Random group: each strand's pull toward its clump
  scaled down at random, so strands don't sit perfectly on their guide (on top of Strength Random,
  per clump). Drags of it evaluate on the CPU for now.

## 0.28.0 (October 23, 2026)

- **Align Photo.** The Photos tab's **Align Photo...** opens the Photos window on the photo: the
  photo on the left, the head mesh on the right, as the Transfer Groom window works. Click a feature
  on the photo and the same feature on the mesh; from four pairs the camera lines up as you click,
  each pair shows its error, and the mesh's outline draws over the photo. No more looking through
  the camera to pick points. The window's **Focal Length**, **Keep focal length** (now kept with
  each photo) and **Lock** are the camera's; right-click a Focal Length slider for **Reset Focal
  Length** if a solve takes the lens somewhere odd. Guides from Photos is the same window's
  **Guides (experimental)** stage. In Maya and the app (Blender keeps Pick Points for now).
- **Switch photos while looking through one.** Picking another photo in the Photos tab while the
  viewport looks through a photo looks through the new one; Back to Perspective still goes back.
- **Subdivide: smooth strands from few points.** A new node: each level doubles every strand's
  segments along a smooth curve through its points, so a curl grown on few points renders round.
  Unlike Resample it doesn't make the nodes before it work on more points: put it last, keep the
  Resample low, and only the output pays for the smoothness. **Levels** for renders and exports
  (2 by default), **Viewport Levels** for the viewport (1).
- **Expand Inputs on graph nodes.** A button on a node's header shows all of its connectable
  params as inputs, or hides them again (connected ones stay). (Thanks to Omar Hesham.)
- **No point budget by default.** The viewport's Point Budget starts at 0 (no limit) in Maya, the
  app and Blender; set one to keep a very large groom interactive.

## 0.27.0 (October 23, 2026)

- **Display Ribbons shows the strands at their true width.** With Display Ribbons on (off by
  default), the strands draw as ribbons facing the camera, as wide as the strands times Ribbon
  Width Scale and never thinner than a pixel; the parts thinner than a pixel fade with their
  width, as the lines do. Turning the camera keeps them facing it. Use it to judge widths and
  density close up (it is slower than lines there). With lighting off, the ribbons now take the
  groom's color as the lines do.
- **Paint modes.** The Paint brush and Fill now **Replace**, **Add**, **Subtract** or **Clamp
  Maximum**, on guide, vertex and texture maps, in Maya, the app and Blender. A stroke adds at most
  its amount however often its dabs overlap. (Thanks to Omar Hesham.)
- **Thinnest Strand for card bakes.** A Cards node setting: the narrowest a strand bakes, in the
  atlas's pixels. 1, as before, keeps every strand solid and at least a pixel wide, so cards cut out
  at a cutoff don't break up, though they read denser than the groom; 0 bakes the strands to
  scale, as renders and the viewport show them, for blended or dithered cards.
- **A Focal Length slider in the Photos tab.** Set the photo camera's lens by hand when you know it;
  Keep focal length turns on, so a solve keeps it.
- **Faster everywhere.** Release builds let the compiler inline more: about 6% off the operators on
  average and 15 to 20% off brush dabs, with the same results to the last bit.

## 0.26.0 (October 22, 2026)

- **Only a Resample on the way to the Output sets the resolution.** A Resample node fed by
  nothing, or one whose strands never reach the Output, still set every strand's point count; now
  only the Resamples the Output's strands pass through do. If a scene of yours has a Resample off to
  the side, its strands come back with the default resolution: wire the Resample into the chain.
- **Feather shafts show their rachis.** The shaft a feather's Propagate keeps is drawn and rendered
  as the rachis tube (Arnold, V-Ray, RenderMan), not as a strand of barb width, and its width no
  longer comes from the Width node. The feather presets now offer **Vane width** and **Barb width**
  in place of Length and Width.
- **Smooth card edges in Maya's viewport.** Textured cards are smoothed by the viewport's
  anti-aliasing instead of a hard cut-out, their shadows follow the card's shape, and ambient
  occlusion no longer draws boxes around them.
- **Guide dots stay behind the scalp.** With a feather's Keep Shaft on, the dots on guides behind the
  scalp no longer showed through it.
- **Faster Merge, Smooth and Clump.** Each takes less time, with the same results to the last bit.

## 0.25.0 (October 22, 2026)

- **Isolate works by group.** Isolate, beside the Group choice in the Groom Tools, dimmed every
  hierarchy level but the picked group's, so with several top-level groups nothing dimmed. It now
  keeps the picked group and its child groups at full color and dims every other group (Maya, the
  app and Blender). On the groom node, **Isolate Group** (`isolateGroup`) replaces Display Level: a
  scene saved with Isolate on shows every group until you pick one again.
- **Scatter's density expressions mask the roots.** With Randomness 0, a density expression let
  roots spread past where it falls to 0 (roots on the neck); on a scalp with Subdivision 2 it gave
  no strands at all. Both fixed.
- **Faster curls.** Curl is about 1.8 times faster (a 500,000-strand groom: 62 to 35 ms), with the
  same curls to the last bit, so clump, noise and brush edits on curly grooms come back sooner.
- **Smoother orbiting.** The viewport's level of detail no longer works its strands out again on
  every frame while the camera turns about the groom.
- **Less memory for Keep Clearance.** Interpolate's skin distance field takes a quarter of the
  memory it did.
- **A new home page.** Every strand on [getgroomlab.com](https://getgroomlab.com) is grown by the
  GroomLab engine itself, running in your browser, and [the groom viewer](/view) opens a `.groom`
  file there, maps and all.

## 0.24.0 (October 21, 2026)

- **GroomLab is developed in the open.** The source lives at
  [github.com/thekesslereffect/groomlab](https://github.com/thekesslereffect/groomlab) and takes
  pull requests: work happens on the `dev` branch, and each release lands on `main`
  ([Contributing](https://github.com/thekesslereffect/groomlab/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Every guide group gets its own color.** Guides are colored per group, child groups included,
  each with a hue of its own that differs per groom; the "by level" mode is gone. Guide Color is
  now **Group** or **Painted Layer** (a painted guide layer, black to white). A color you pick for a
  group still wins.
- **Hand-placed flight feathers.** GroomLab > Add > Feathers offers **Scattered feathers** or **One
  feather per guide (hand-placed)**: pick a guide group (`feathers`, `flight_left`, `tail`...) and
  each of its guides is exactly one feather, with none in between. The new **Flight feathers
  (hand-placed)** preset makes the group and switches to the Draw tool on it
  ([Hand-placed flight feathers](/docs/fur-and-feathers#hand-placed-flight-feathers)).
- **The app: the scalp hides the hair behind it.** In the lookdev view on Direct3D 11, strands on
  the far side of the scalp showed through it; fixed.
- **A new look for the site.** The website and the GroomLab wordmark are set in Google Sans Flex.

## 0.23.0 (October 21, 2026)

- **One current groom.** The Groom Tools and the Graph Editor always show the same groom: picking
  one in either, or selecting a groom, makes it current for both, and brushes work on it. Pin the
  Graph Editor (its pin button) to keep it on one groom while the Groom Tools move on. The Groom
  Tools' "Selected groom / follow selection" row is gone: selecting a groom always makes it current
  ([The current groom and what shows](/docs/guides-and-brushes#the-current-groom-and-what-shows)).
- **Show the hair or the guides.** The Groom Tools header has two eyes: one hides the hair alone
  (strands, cards, scales, instances; the viewport only, renders keep it), the other the guides
  alone. Shift+click either for every groom in the scene; GroomLab > Display has Show/Hide All Hair
  and Show/Hide All Guides, and Alt+Shift+G toggles the guides. In Maya the hair's eye is the new
  shape attribute `displayHair`; hide a whole groom as any node (Ctrl+H).
- **Each groom's guides get their own colors.** Guide groups are colored automatically from a hue
  that differs per groom, so two grooms no longer share the same blue; a group's own color still
  wins.
- **Delete All Flow Arrows.** GroomLab > Utilities > Delete All Flow Arrows clears a groom's painted
  arrows and sinks in one undo step, and the Flow tool shows how many there are
  ([Delete All Flow Arrows](/docs/utilities#delete-all-flow-arrows)).
- **Node actions at the top.** A node's buttons (the Cards node's exports, Simulate Range and Bake,
  Bake Node to Guides, Clear Sculpt Edits, a compound's Save) sit at the top of its settings, no
  scrolling needed.
- **A shorter Groom Tools.** Sections show when the tool at hand uses them: Tubes with Place, Draw,
  Shape and Select, the sculpting options with the brushes that sculpt.
- **The app: Properties tabs, a smooth scalp.** The Properties dock has tabs (Attributes,
  Material, Lighting, Render); GroomLab > Studio lights the viewport without leaving Groom mode;
  lookdev starts Interactive over a gradient, and lighting presets keep your background.
  Properties > Inputs > Scalp Subdivision grooms on the Catmull-Clark smoothed scalp
  ([Scalp Subdivision](/docs/app#scalp-subdivision)). The Dyed swatch dyes the hair violet; the
  GroomLab swatch puts the default pigment back.

## 0.22.0 (October 21, 2026)

- **The app looks and works like GroomLab in Maya.** The standalone app has a menu bar with the same

- **No freeze on OpenGL.** The first GPU stroke after an update used to stall for up to a minute
  while the graphics driver compiled GroomLab's GPU code. It now compiles in the background when
  a groom first draws (brushing stays on the CPU until it's ready) and is cached, so later
  sessions start at once.
- **A smoother app.** Orbiting and parameter drags hold 60 fps, a CPU brush stroke no longer slows
  the window (strokes and commits run off the UI thread), and the first frame shows in a quarter
  of a second instead of four. Click a tool's name as well as its icon.
- **Placing guides freezes nothing.** Solo new guides is now an option on Place and Draw, off by
  default; **Shift+click** with a brush still solos a guide.

## 0.2.1 (October 11, 2026)

- **No more hang on the first groom.** Creating the first groom of a Maya session no longer
  freezes Maya for several seconds: the hair shader compiles in a quarter second, once.
- **New brushes.** **Direction** turns guides toward the way you stroke. **Noise** adds jitter
  that builds up with each stroke (Ctrl smooths it out). **Width** and **Density** paint how thick
  the hair is and where it grows; the first stroke makes and wires the map. Works in Maya, the app
  and Blender. See [Guides & brushes](/docs/guides-and-brushes#brushes).
- **Brush curves.** **Root to tip** sets how much of the brush reaches each part of a guide: style
  the ends and leave the roots alone. **Falloff curve** draws the brush's edge.
- **Symmetry on the scalp itself.** Symmetry can mirror across the scalp's own left and right
  (**Topology**), not only world X: right for a head that is turned or not quite symmetric.
- **Solo guides.** **Shift+click** a guide with a brush to solo it (the rest freeze, so you shape
  only it); **Shift+click** off a guide, or **Unfreeze All** beside
  Freeze, to free them all. The Select tool works the same way, and symmetry keeps the mirror
  guides live too.
- **Several scalps on one groom.** Ears, a head and a body, or eyebrow patches, in one groom:
  **Pick** on the **Scalps** row of the Attribute Editor's Inputs. See
  [Node graph](/docs/node-graph#several-scalps).
- **Library looks.** The **Reference** node brings in another groom file's strands or guides.
  See [Node graph](/docs/node-graph#linked-groom-node).
- **Render overrides.** `renderDensity`, `renderWidthScale` and `renderLengthScale` change the
  render without touching the viewport. See [Rendering](/docs/rendering#render-overrides).
- **Viewport fixes.** Cards are shaded like the strands they stand for, light hair stays bright
  with the light behind the camera, and hair shading works with anti-aliasing off.

## October 10, 2026

- **Clump centers from a dropdown.** Clump's `centersFrom` says where its centers come from:
  `pick` (it picks its own among the strands), `inputPoints` or `inputCurves` (whatever you feed
  its `centers` input). Connecting `centers` switches it to `inputPoints`. A low-density Scatter

  [Node graph](/docs/node-graph#clump). *Grooms saved before:* it replaces

- **No hidden params.** A param never hides because an input is connected; what a panel shows
  follows its dropdowns only.
- **Uneven ends with Length.** `fraction` picks a share of the strands, and each loses a random
  share of its length between `trimMin` and `trimMax`. *Grooms saved before:* it replaces
  `randomness`; set `trimMax` to the old `randomness` for a close look.
- **Looping Wind and Jiggle.** Turn on `loop` and set `loopStart` and `loopEnd` for a seamless
  cycle: game idles and looping clips. See
  [Motion & simulation](/docs/motion-and-simulation#looping-animation).
- **Curl spacing.** `frequencyProfile` tightens or loosens the curls along the strand, and

  colliders) wait for you to click the mesh in the viewport or the Outliner. Scalp and
  ColliderMesh nodes in the Graph Editor have a **Mesh** field with the same eyedropper.

  grooms.

## October 9, 2026

- **GroomLab's own hair shading in the viewport.** Strands in Maya's viewport are lit with
  Marschner's three highlights from their pigment and RenderData's specular and roughness,
  calibrated against Arnold's `aiStandardHair`. The hair shadows itself, light scatters through it,
  and each strand shades a little apart from its neighbours. The standalone app draws the same
  look. See [Rendering](/docs/rendering#viewport-shading).
- **Clump level of detail.** Seen from afar, each clump draws only as many strands as cover its
  pixels: big grooms draw several times faster, and closer to the render. Renders and exports
  always take every strand.
- **Curl rebuilt.** One curve at any resolution, with no kink at the root: 40 or 256 segments make
  the same curl. Where a strand has too few points for a turn, Curl adds them.
- **The GPU previews most of the graph.** Dragging a slider or brushing guides draws on the GPU
  through Interpolate, Length, Clump, Noise, Curl, Width, Part, Gravity, Flyaway and Collide;
  letting go commits on the CPU, so renders and exports are unchanged.
- **No stall on release.** Commits run off the UI thread, and the GPU preview stays on screen
  until they land.
- **Painted maps without seams.** Brushing and smoothing cross UV seams, and a dab stays on the
  surface it lands on (never the back of an ear). See [Painting maps](/docs/painting-maps).
- **Lighter at a million strands.** Interpolate's memory at a million strands went from 208 MB to
  37 MB, and Follow Scalp playback is carried by the GPU.

## October 7, 2026

- **One naming scheme.** A node's random spread is `randomness`, a param's own spread is
  `<param>Random`, and every node says `strength`, `along` and `direction`, `profile` and
  `clumpChannel` the same way.
- **Fewer, clearer nodes.** Bend stands strands up at the root, Clump does all the clumping, and
  Map is the one map node. HairColor, Bands and FeatherColor split the color work; RenderData holds the
  highlight (`specular`, `roughness`) and the opacity (`opacityProfile`). Instance and Scales are
  separate nodes, and Length only trims (Transform's `scale` lengthens). See
  [Node graph](/docs/node-graph#node-categories).
- **Strands stay out of the scalp.** Interpolate's **Keep clearance** keeps every strand at least
  as high above the scalp as its guides.
- **Regions.** Paint areas in a color each and name the map in Interpolate's `regionMap`: strands
  blend only their own region's guides. See [Regions](/docs/node-graph#regions).
- **Right at any scale.** Every length param fits the scalp's size, Maya's sizes are in
  centimeters whatever the UI's unit, and a new groom's default graph is fitted to its scalp.
- **Straighten.** A node that pulls strands toward a straight line from the root, lengths kept.
