## Load one

In the Graph Editor, **Presets** lists the presets by category. Hover a preset for a one-line
description; pick it to load its graph, laid out left to right (backdrops label the branches of
the technique presets).

- **Scaled to the scalp.** The hair presets are built on a 20 cm head, the fur presets on a
  wolf-sized body (100 cm). On a larger or smaller scalp, lengths and widths are scaled with its
  size, densities with its area, and curl frequency (turns per centimeter) inversely, so the look
  is the same on any size of scalp.
- **Clumps sized by distance.** Clumps are sized in centimeters (Clump's `clumpBy` `size`), so a
  whole body gets more clumps than a head at the same clump size.
- **Guides required.** Most presets interpolate from your guides; place or comb a few first. The
  fur presets marked "no guides" follow flow arrows instead.
- **Descriptive node names.** For example, `frizzNoise` is a Noise applied to a fraction of the
  strands (frizz is a Noise setting, not a separate node); `curlsControl` is a slider on the
  groom.
- **Insert instead of replace.** Each preset's submenu has **Apply** (replaces the graph) and
  **Insert After Selected Node**: the preset's look (every node between its sources and its
  Output) is inserted as one Compound after the selected node (or before the Output), scaled to
  your scalp, in one undo step. The **Add-ons** below are small looks meant for this.
- **Exposed controls.** An inserted Compound shows the look's main parameters on its own panel
  (clump strength and count, noise, curl, color, width, depending on the preset), with the same
  groups, units and sliders as the nodes inside. Edit, key or save them like any parameter; they
  set the nodes inside.

## Hair

| Preset | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `hair_basics` | The basic chain: Scatter, Interpolate, soft clumps, a little noise, brown color, tapered width. |
| `cinematic_hair` | A detailed hero look: large locks with finer clumps inside them, restrained noise, frizz on a few strands near the tips, a few flyaways, uneven lengths and widths, color variation. |
| `curly` | Ringlets: small clumps curled together (Curl with `coherence` `clump`), a little frizz, and the segments curls need. |
| `braids` | Box braids without drawn paths: the Braid node places one braid per 2.6 cm of scalp (`braidSpacing`). |
| `braids_beaded` | Box braids sealed at the ends with beads and cuffs near the tips: the braid's ties and an Instance node on them (`beadsPerBraid`, `beadSpacing`, `cinch`). |
| `braids_curly_ends` | Box braids tied off near the end, the ends below the tie curling: a Curl mapped by the Braid's `braidTail` channel (`curlyLength`, `curlFrequency`). |
| `braids_twists` | Two-strand twists all over the head: the Braid's rope style (`plies` 3 for three-strand twists). |
| `braids_cornrows` | Cornrows along rows you draw: each row picks up the hair on its sides into every crossing, hanging as braids past `onTheHeadUntil`, a bead at each end. |
| `braids_french` | A French braid along one path you draw down the back, fed from both sides, tied off at the end (`dutch` for a Dutch braid). |
| `braids_loose` | A loose braid along one path you draw: pulled apart (`pancake`), sections looping out of it and back (`loops`, `loopSize`). |
| `braids_fishtail_ponytail` | A fishtail ponytail from one path you draw from its base: all the hair sweeps into it, tied at the base and the end. |
| `parted_style` | A part drawn with the Part tool, respected by Interpolate and Clump. |
| `tube_locks` | Tube locks (anime): stylized locks shaped as a whole. Interpolate's Fill Tubes (with a soft edge) fills each guide's tube, soft clumps follow the tubes, a little noise, tapered tips. Place guides, then shape their tubes with the Shape brush (see [Tube guides](/docs/guides-and-brushes#tube-guides)). |

## Fur

| Preset | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `creature_fur` | Fur for a whole animal, laid by flow arrows (no guides): soft tufts about a centimeter across, lighter tips. |
| `agouti_fur` | The creature fur in wild-type bands (a Bands node after HairColor): dark roots, a pale band, black tips, a plain undercoat. |
| `white_wool` | Dense, crimped white fleece in soft locks: a picked off-white (HairColor's `color` mode), white tips. |
| `flow_with_guides` | Fur along the flow, and your guides (clumped) where you paint the `guideAreas` map white: tufts, a ruff, a mane. The map is made for you, black (all fur). |

## Feathers

`feathers_contour`, `feathers_down`, `feathers_flight`, `feathers_afterfeather` and
`feathers_barred` (bars, iridescence and a downy base: [Color patterns](/docs/color-patterns)):
one per design of the Feathers tool, each as a Compound with its main params exposed.
`feathers_afterfeather` shows branching inside a design: the vane, a fluffy base and its barbules
are three Propagates on the same shafts, each with its own HairColor, joined by Merge.
`feathers_spotted`, `feathers_eye_spot` and `feathers_iridescent` are stylized color patterns of
the barred design. `layered_feathers` lays the contour design's feathers over one another and off
the body with a Plume node. `hand_placed_feathers` (**Flight feathers (hand-placed)**) starts a
wing or tail by hand: it makes an empty `feathers` guide group and picks the Draw tool drawing into
it, and every guide you draw is one flight feather
([Hand-placed flight feathers](/docs/fur-and-feathers#hand-placed-flight-feathers)). See
[Fur & feathers](/docs/fur-and-feathers).

## Scales

| Preset | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `dragon_scales` | Scales placed by strands: combed down the body, larger on the back, green with darker tips, keeled plates on the back and shields on the belly, overlapping and conformed to the skin. |
| `scales_from_guides` | Scales pointed by your guides through Interpolate: comb or brush the guides and the scales turn with them. A Bend lays them down the body until you place guides. |
| `scales_from_flow` | Scales pointed by flow arrows, no guides: draw flow arrows with the Flow tool and the scales follow. |
| `swept_spikes` | Spikes or quills that bend with the guides: long, narrow plates in the Scales node's `follow` `strand`, based on the skin and bent along their strands' curves. |

## Techniques

| Preset | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `expression_driven` | Expressions: longer hair at the crown (a keyable variable), harder clumps on long strands. |
| `painted_maps` | One color map, three masks: R clumps, G frizz, B length. The map is made for you. |
| `blend_two_looks` | Blend mixes a sleek and a curly look; its `mix` is the keyable `ctl_curls`. Key it to animate. |
| `merge_layers` | Layers, compounds and switches: a sparse layer of longer, wavy strands with its own Scatter (its waves packed in a Compound with its own sliders), merged onto the base; a Switch picks the full look or a light one. |
| `vine_leaves` | The Instance node: leaves along curling vines, each bent along its vine (`deform` `alongStrand`). Pick your leaf with Prototype from Selection. |

## Add-ons

Small looks meant for **Insert After Selected Node**; each adds one effect to an existing groom.
They can also be applied on their own.

| Preset | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| `wet` | Tight clumps and a darker color, for hair just out of water. |
| `frizz` | Kinky noise on a share of the strands, growing toward the tips. |
| `flyaways` | A few strands leave the flow near the tip and stand out. |
| `cards_mid` | A Cards node at the mid level of detail; insert it before the Output. |
| `gravity` | Strands hang under their weight, stiff at the root (a Collide after it keeps them off the scalp). |
| `two_tone_tips` | A picked root color (a skin tone) blending to white tips, each strand a little lighter or darker ([Color patterns](/docs/color-patterns#white-hair-and-tips-a-different-color)). |

## Save your own

**Presets** > **Save as Preset...** saves the graph, its layout and its compounds to your
presets folder. Your presets appear at the top level of the menu. From Python:

```python
from groomlab import presets
presets.save_preset(groom, "my_look", about={"title": "My look", "category": "Hair",
                                             "description": "Wavy, for the lead."})
presets.apply_preset(other_groom, "my_look")
```

Studios can share presets through the `GROOMLAB_PRESET_PATH` environment variable.

### Choose what an inserted preset shows

A preset file may list the params its Compound shows when inserted, in an `"exposed"` field:

```json
"exposed": [
  {"node": "clump", "param": "strength", "name": "clumpStrength"},
  {"node": "color", "param": "melanin", "name": "melanin"},
  {"node": "width", "param": "width"}
]
```

Each entry names a node of the look and one of its params; `name` is what the Compound calls it
(the param's own name when left out, or node and param together, `clumpStrength`, when the
Compound has that name already). Entries naming a node outside the look (its Scatter or Guides)
are skipped. The panel lists them in the look's order, each with its node's slider range, unit
and group. Every shipped preset with a look lists 1 to 8.

A preset without the list (for example one saved from a graph) exposes a default selection: one
param per look node for up to six nodes, prioritizing clumps, curls, noise, braids, tangles,
flyaways, gravity, combs, length, color and width, and using each node's first plain numeric
param (not an advanced one, and not one driven by a map, a port or a key).

## Share a look as text

**Presets** > **Copy Graph** copies the groom's graph to the clipboard as JSON text, in the same
format Save as Preset writes, titled after the groom, in the "Shared" category, plus its control
values (`"controls"`). It can be pasted in a chat, a forum or an email.

- **Paste Graph** puts copied text on the groom shown, fitted to its scalp, in one undo step.
  Text in a code block (```` ```json ````) works too.
- **Saved as a `.json` file** in a presets folder, the text is a preset like any other: it
  shows under Shared, and loading it sets the control values too.
- **Not included:** painted maps, keys, referenced files and scene meshes are not part of the
  text. The status bar lists what the target groom lacks; those nodes use their defaults.

```python
text = groomlab.copy_graph_text(groom)
report = groomlab.paste_graph_text(other_groom, text)
print(report["message"])
```
