---

<Example name="reference.png" alt="A reference photo lined up with the head mesh" caption="Groom Tools > Photos" optional />

The **Photos** tab in the Groom Tools creates a camera for each reference photo, aligned to the
head mesh, so you can groom while looking through it.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Import a photo

**Import Photo...** creates a camera aimed at the head with the photo on its image plane. The
photo is copied next to the scene, so it moves with the scene.

</Step>
<Step>

### Look through it

**Look Through** shows the photo's camera in the active viewport. Click it again to return to the
previous camera.

</Step>
<Step>

### Line it up

**Align Photo...** opens the **Photos** window on the photo: the photo on the left, the head mesh on
the right, like the Transfer Groom window. You never have to look through the camera to do it.

1. Click a feature on the photo (the corner of an eye, the tip of the nose, an ear), then the same
   feature on the mesh, or the other way round. The Nth point on the photo pairs with the Nth on the
   mesh, numbered on both sides. Drag a point to adjust it; Ctrl+click deletes it and its partner.
2. From four pairs the camera lines up as you place them (the focal length kept). Six or more,
   spread in depth, solve the focal length too, unless **Keep focal length** is on.
3. If you know the lens, set **Focal Length** (mm): Keep focal length turns on and the camera
   lines up again keeping it.

The mesh's outline draws over the photo through the camera, and each pair shows its error in pixels
(a line to where its mesh point lands). A pair that disagrees with the rest turns red: fix that one
first. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo in the window. Below **Align Photo...**, the tab says how
far along the photo is.

You can also line it up by eye: look through it and move the camera.

</Step>
<Step>

### Morph the scalp to the photos (optional)

When the head mesh is not the person in the photos, **Morph to Photos** (in the Photos window,
under **Morph**) shapes the scalp so every lined-up photo's pairs land on their photo points. A
pair seen in one photo moves across that photo's view; a pair seen in two or more photos gets its
depth from them too. The rest of the mesh follows smoothly.

- **Symmetry** (on by default) morphs both sides alike, each pair mirrored across the scalp's
  middle.
- **Stiffness** keeps more of the scalp's own shape: 0 lands every pair exactly, higher moves the
  scalp more as one piece and the pairs fall short.
- Each press starts from the scalp as it was before the first morph, so pressing it again after
  moving a pair never stacks morphs. **Reset Morph** puts the scalp back (undoable).

In Maya the morph is a blendShape on the scalp; in Blender (the Photos tab) a shape key over the
Basis. In the app the groom moves onto the morphed scalp by its UVs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Lock and groom

**Lock** the camera once it is aligned (in the tab or the Photos window): the landmarks no longer
move it, nor does tumbling through it. Set the photo's **Opacity**, place it **In front** of the
mesh or **Behind** it, and brush the guides to match the photo while looking through the camera.

</Step>
</Steps>

```python
import groomlab.reference as ref

cam = ref.import_reference("C:/photos/front.jpg", groom=groom)
ref.set_pair(cam, 0, image=(412, 230), mesh=(0.0, 9.5, 3.1))   # ... 6 pairs (4 keeping the focal length)
ref.solve(cam)          # the camera from the pairs (the Photos window does it as you click)
ref.morph_to_photos(groom, stiffness=0.0, symmetric=True)   # optional; ref.reset_photo_morph(groom)
ref.look_through(cam)
ref.lock_reference(cam)
ref.set_reference_opacity(cam, 0.4)
```

## Guides from photos (experimental)

<Callout type="info" title="Experimental">
Guides from Photos grows guides that follow the hair in your reference photos. It is a first
version: try it, then brush the result.
</Callout>

**GroomLab > Guides from Photos (experimental)...** opens the Photos window at its **Guides** stage
(Groom Tools > Photos has an **Open Guides from Photos...** button too; the **Align** and **Guides
(experimental)** chips at the top left switch stages). The Guides stage adds flow arrows, mask
touch-ups, Generate and the measured look to the Align stage's landmarks. Each photo keeps its camera
with the photo on an image plane, lined up, so renders and Arnold can use it.

<Example name="reference-window.png" alt="The Photos window at its Guides stage: a photo with numbered landmarks, flow arrows and the mesh's outline on the left, the scalp with the matching landmarks on the right" caption="The Photos window, Guides stage" optional />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the photos

**Import Photo...** adds a photo as a tab along the top (**Remove** takes it away; Maya's undo brings
it back). One to three views work: a front, a side and a back, or a single three-quarter view with
symmetry. Each photo has its own landmarks, arrows, mask and camera. In the photo view the wheel
zooms at the cursor, the middle button (or Alt+drag) pans, and **F** or **Fit** fits the photo.

</Step>
<Step>

### Line each photo up with landmarks

With **Landmarks** on, line each photo up as in [Line it up](#line-it-up) above. The mesh can draw
over the photo as its outline or its wireframe (**Mesh over photo**, with its **Opacity**).

</Step>
<Step>

### Draw flow arrows (optional)

With **Flow** on, drag along the hair on the photo from root to tip: each stroke becomes an arrow,
and guides near it follow its line and its way. Ctrl+drag erases. **Clear Arrows** forgets them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Touch up the mask (optional)

The guides grow where the photo's hair mask says there is hair: automatic on a plain backdrop, or an
image you pick with **Mask** (white where the hair is). **Mask tint** shows it over the photo and
**Flow field** the strand directions read from it. Paint with **Hair** where it missed hair and with
**Backdrop** where it took backdrop for hair (Ctrl swaps them; **Brush** sets the size).

Every landmark, arrow and touch-up is one undo step: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y in the window (or **Undo**
and **Redo**) walk the current photo's edits.

</Step>
<Step>

### Generate

Set **Symmetry** (Auto uses the scalp's own symmetry), **Guide count** and **Max length** (Auto: as
far as the photos show the hair reaching), uncheck **Use this photo** on a photo to leave it out, then
**Generate**. The guides go into the **fromReference** group and are selected. Generate again
replaces them; one Maya undo step each time. While it works, the window shows a wait cursor, greys
out its buttons and says what it is doing (Measured Look's Apply too).

**Measured Look** shows what the photos measure against what your graph has: the hair color (and
lighter or darker tips), clumps (how far apart and how tight), curls, flyaways, frizz, and where the
hair thins. Uncheck what you don't want and click **Apply**: the values are set in one undo step, and
a Clump, Curl, frizz Noise or Flyaway node the graph lacks is added where it usually goes. Thinning
becomes a vertex map (refDensity) on Scatter's density.

These are rough measures from the picture, a starting point to tune by eye. Some things can't be read
from a photo yet: each strand's own color variation (it hides in the shading), frizz in curly hair,
clumps in short fur, and flyaways against a backdrop of the hair's own shade.

</Step>
</Steps>

The standalone app has the same window: **Align Photo...** in its Photos tab, and **Tools > Guides
from Photos (Experimental)...** (photos, landmarks, the camera, flow arrows, mask touch-ups and
Generate, saved with the groom; the measured look is Maya's for now).

What works so far: long hair and longer fur, one to three views, symmetry that covers the side a
photo doesn't show, and flow arrows that settle which way hair runs.

What doesn't yet: tight curls in three or more views, very short fur (too fine to read in the
photo), and photos lit by colored light (the fitted color takes the light's tint).

```python
import groomlab
from groomlab import from_reference as fr
from groomlab.ui import reference_window

w = reference_window.show(groom, stage="guides")  # the Photos window; scripts drive it too:
cam = w.import_photo("C:/photos/front.jpg")
w.add_landmark("photo", (412, 230)); w.add_landmark("mesh", (0.0, 9.5, 3.1))   # ... 4 pairs or more
w.add_flow_stroke([(820, 300), (790, 620)])        # photo pixels, root first

fr.set_reference_hints(groom, cam, [[(820, 300), (790, 620)]])   # or straight onto the groom
fr.set_mask_strokes(groom, cam, [{"points": [(100, 80), (180, 90)], "radius": 12, "include": False}])
result = fr.generate(groom, guide_count=400, symmetry="auto")
fr.measured_look_preview(groom)                  # [{"type", "node", "param", "current", "value"}, ...]
fr.apply_measured_look(groom)                    # or types=["HairColor", "Clump"]
```

Scripts can also pass a painted overlay: a PNG at the photo's size with the strokes opaque on a
transparent layer, dark at the root end and light at the tip (`{"camera": cam, "hint_image":
"flow.png"}` in `groomlab.guides_from_reference`).
