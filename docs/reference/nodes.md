# Nodes

Every operator by category, then the look, shape and deform operators one row each.

User guide: [Node graph](https://www.getgroomlab.com/docs/node-graph).

## Categories

- **Sources:** `Scalp` (the groom's scalp, or another of its scalps), `Guides`, `ColliderMesh` (any other mesh: a
  collider), `ImportCurves`, `Reference` (shown as **Linked Groom**: another groom file's strands or
  guides, a library look).
- **Grow:** `Scatter`, `Interpolate`, `Cull`.
- **Shape:** `Length`, `Width`, `Resample`, `Direction`, `Flow` (short hair along painted flow
  arrows), `Part`, `Smooth`, `Mirror`, `RestoreLength`, `Sculpt` (brush the guides the graph made:
  see [Sculpting generated guides](nodes-and-fields.md#sculpting-generated-guides)), `Subdivide`
  (smooth strands from few points).
- **Deform:** `Bend`, `Transform`, `BlendNeighbors`, `Gravity`, `Displace`.
- **Collide:** `Collide`, `SelfCollide` (hair no longer through itself), `MeshCut`.
- **Look:** `Clump`, `Twist`, `Braid`, `Noise`, `Curl`, `Flyaway`, `Static` (charged hair puffing out), `Tangle`, `Wet` (wet hair from one slider), `Instance`, `Scales`.
- **Convert:** `ToGuides`, `ToStrands`, `StrandMesh`. **Games:** `Cards`.
- **Color:** `HairColor`, `Bands`, `FeatherColor`, `RenderData`.
- **Motion:** `Wind`, `Jiggle` (no simulation cache: see [Motion without a cache](motion-and-simulation.md#motion-without-a-cache)), `Simulate`
  (the guides simulated, every host: see [Simulating the guides](motion-and-simulation.md#simulating-the-guides)).
- **Feathers:** `Propagate` (barbs grown off shafts), `Plume` (feathers laid over one another and off the body), `Split` (strands routed to four outputs).
- **Utility:** `Map`, `Attribute`, `Group`, `Merge`, `Switch`, `Blend`, `Output`; `Compound` and `Input` for compound nodes.
- **Fields:** `Control` (a keyable slider on the groom), `Expression`, `FieldNoise`, `FieldRemap`, `FieldCombine`, `Curve`.

## Operators

| Operator | What it does | Main parameters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Clump` | Pulls each strand toward its nearest center; writes a `clumpId` channel (`clumpChannel`): each clump's number, from its center's ID (not its place in a list), so an edit in one place renumbers no clump elsewhere. `centersFrom` says where its centers come from: `pick` (the default) picks its own among the strands (a connected `centers` is ignored, and the node says so); `inputPoints` and `inputCurves` take the `centers` input, guides or strands (a low-density Scatter straight in, a guide level, ToGuides; of strands, their core: all of a scatter of under 5,000; nothing connected: the strands pass through, and the node says so). Connecting `centers` in an editor sets `inputPoints`; disconnecting leaves it. `inputPoints` takes only the centers' roots, to place the clumps (a single point will do), and each axis is the core strand the clump gathers nearest its center's root (else the core strand rooted nearest it), the hair as grown there (XGen's clump guides), so scattered points clump long, bent hair without flattening it onto the points; `inputCurves` follows the center curves themselves (groomed clump curves, a Noise waving the centers, whiskers to part around). Each strand's point goes toward the axis's point as far along it as the point is along the strand; `pastTip` says what a strand longer than its axis does past the axis's tip: `gather` (the default) at the tip, `extend` on along the axis's last direction, so long hair keeps its length. With `pick` (Focus on it shows the strands with its centers as dots on the scalp; these params show only with `pick`). `clumpBy` picks how the clumps are sized, and the UIs show only the param it picks: `count` (the default: about that many, whatever the scalp's size: the spacing is the rest scalp's area over the count, in 1% steps, so it re-fits to the scalp and an edit that changes the area by more than a step moves every clump) or `size` (a length: clumps about that far apart wherever strands grow; the count follows the area they cover, so a whole body gets more clumps than a head, each the same size; an edit of the scalp changes the clumps only near it; on feathers, as many along each vane as fit); a preset's size is fitted like any length. `centerWeight` (mappable) is each strand's chance to be a center, e.g. an expression for long strands only. The centers are picked locally: darts are thrown on the rest scalp (each face's from its own area and `seed`) and each lands on the nearest root, then each dart in a stable random order becomes a center unless one already taken is near it, so changing the Scatter's density or seed moves the clumps a little instead of rerolling them, and an edit of the scalp moves only the clumps around it; `randomness` is Scatter's: 0 keeps the centers 0.7 of the spacing apart (evenly spread), 1 lets them land anywhere, about as many either way; the centers come from the core strands (the ones every preview holds), so every preview shows the render's clumps. With a `lockRadius`, strands gather into a round lock of that thickness instead (dreads, wet strings); a `Twist` node after it turns the locks into ropes. A negative `strength` (to -1) pushes strands away from their centers instead: points within `repelRadius` of the center's point as far along it move out toward that distance (all the way at -1 for points on the center, easing off to none at the radius; strands beyond it untouched), across the center and never into the skin, lengths kept, the `profile` applying as ever: fur parting around whiskers, horns or eyes, their guides as the `centers` with `centersFrom` `inputCurves` (with `inputPoints` the push is from the strands nearest them, and the node says so). `flatness` squashes each clump across into a ribbon as much as it clumps (wet hair), lying along the scalp (`flatAlong` `surface`), turned at random per clump (`random`) or with its width along `flatDirection` (`direction`). Per clump (by its center's ID and the `seed`, as XGen and Yeti vary clumps; all 0 by default): `volumize` (mappable) pushes each clump's strands out from its axis by that share of their distance from it, most near the root along `volumizeProfile` (1 to 0), filling the clump's volume; `strengthRandom` scales each clump's strength down by up to that share and `lockRadiusRandom` its lock radius up or down; `copyAmount` blends each clump's center curve toward the mean shape of its `copyNeighbors` nearest clumps (their curves from their roots), so neighbouring clumps share one flow. The GPU preview leaves a Clump with any of these to the CPU. What simpler nodes do is theirs: a **Noise** on its centers with `centersFrom` `inputCurves` (`ToGuides -> Noise -> Clump.centers`), or a Noise before the Clump (its own centers are strands as they come in), waves each clump as one (clump noise); a **Twist** after it spirals the clumps (ropes, locs); a **RestoreLength** after it, its `reference` the Clump's input, keeps every strand's length (Yeti's maintain length); a `strength` varying per strand (an expression such as `rand(7) < 0.03 ? 0.2 : 1`) lets a few strands escape their clump, and a **Flyaway** after it makes strays. After an Interpolate with a `regionMap`, strands clump only toward centers of their own region (`regionChannel`, `region` by default: a center's region is its strand's for centers of its own, its own value of the channel for centers that carry it, else the region of the strand rooted nearest it); a region without a center stays unclumped | `strength` (-1 to 1), `profile` (ramp), `centersFrom`, `clumpBy`, `count`, `size`, `randomness`, `centerWeight` (mappable), `pastTip`, `clumpChannel`, `regionChannel`, `seed`, `lockRadius`, `repelRadius`, `flatness`, `flatAlong`, `flatDirection`; Shape: `volumize` (mappable), `volumizeProfile`, `copyAmount`, `copyNeighbors`; Random: `strengthRandom`, `lockRadiusRandom` |
| `Noise` | Smooth displacement; `strand` space (default) is independent per strand, `world` space is coherent across the groom. On a `fraction` of the strands (mappable) at a high `frequency` with one octave, it is frizz. `mode` `kinky` makes it jagged instead: random offsets at knots `frequency` to the strand's length (strand space) or to a unit of length (world space, the knots' offsets from the noise at the root, so neighbours kink alike), straight between them: sharp kinks, scraggly or frizzy hair | `amplitude`, `frequency`, `octaves`, `profile`, `space`, `fraction`, `mode` |
| `Curl` | Curls around each strand: a `helix` (the default), a planar `wave`, a `zigzag` of sharp kinks (kinky hair) or a `coil`, a tight helix `coilWidths` strand widths across (coily 4A-4C hair: it reads the widths the strands carry, so put a Width before it; a note says when they have none). Every curl starts at `phase` (turns) plus a random share of a turn (`phaseRandom`: 1, the default, any phase; 0, all alike), eased in from the root over its first quarter turn. `frequencyProfile` scales the turns per unit length along the strand (a ramp, root to tip, a share of `frequency`: curls loosening or tightening toward the tip) and `spacingRandom` (0-1) varies the spacing smoothly along each strand (a noise of its own a turn or two long; each clump's with `coherence` `clump`): the curl turns at the local frequency `frequency · frequencyProfile · (1 + spacingRandom · n)`, its angle that integrated along the strand's arc length in closed form, so it never turns back and stays the same curl at any resolution (`curl_phase.h`). `weight` (0, the default: none) stretches the turns where hair hangs below them, as a real ringlet carries the hair beneath it: the load at a point is how much the strand hangs along `weightDirection` (world space, down by default: max(0, tangent · direction), smoothed a little) times the curl's turns from there to the tip, and the local pitch grows by `1 + weight · load / 10` (at weight 1, ten turns hanging straight below double it), so curls open near the root, keep their spacing at the tip, stretch more on long ringlets than short curls and barely on hair lying flat or pointing up; each turn keeps its wire length, so the radius narrows (`r' = sqrt(r² - (q² - 1) / (2π f)²)` for a pitch stretched q-fold at f turns a unit, of the curl's own radius before `profile`, eased smoothly to 0 in its last stretch) and an overstretched curl goes straight. `coherence` `clump` curls each clump as one around its middle (the mean of its core strands, the ones every preview holds; a clump without one curls strand by strand), with one phase, frequency and plane (ringlets; after a Clump, by its `clumpChannel`). The curl is a curve of the strand's shape, not of its points (around a smooth axis through them, by its arc length, in its rotation-minimizing frames): any resolution makes the same curl. Where a strand's points are too few a turn for the `tolerance` (how far a segment may stray from the round curl, a share of the radius: 0.5, the default, 3 a turn, so no curl aliases; 0.1, 7), Curl first cuts each segment evenly along the smooth strand, as many as its longest segment needs at the most the curl turns (frequency, randomness, frequencyProfile and spacingRandom at their most), at most `maxSegments` a strand (a zigzag more than 2 / tolerance a turn, keeping its kinks' peaks; 0: the points coming in; a note says how many strands got points, and a Curl that added some is the CPU's in a GPU preview) | `shape`, `radius` (mappable), `frequency` (turns per unit length), `frequencyProfile`, `randomness`, `spacingRandom`, `profile`, `coilWidths`, `phase`, `phaseRandom`, `weight`, `weightDirection`, `coherence`, `clumpChannel`, `tolerance`, `maxSegments`, `seed` |
| `Flyaway` | Stray hairs: a fraction of strands leave the flow from `start` on in slow arcs, a little longer; writes a `flyaway` channel (1 for strays; an earlier Flyaway's strays keep their 1). Put it after Clump. Strays are picked by strand ID and `seed`, so two Flyaways need different seeds (a new node gets one) | `fraction`, `stray` (both mappable), `frequency`, `start`, `standOut`, `lengthen` |
| `Static` | Hair charged with static repels itself, like dry winter hair: it lifts off the head, flares out toward the tips, and clumped locks fray into their strands. A closed form plus one solve, not a simulation. Every strand carries charge along its length by `profile` (rising toward the tips by default: dry ends carry most), some strands more than others by `randomness` (by ID). The core strands' charge (those every preview holds, at most about 8k of them; a strand's share of the scalp's area, not its width) is splatted into a sparse grid shared with SelfCollide. The electric potential is solved there (red-black SOR, `-lap phi = rho`) over the nodes within 4 cells of hair. The padding takes the potential of all the charge seen from far away (a softened point charge at its middle: hair inside a hollow of hair, such as the hanging part under the head, is pushed out, not balanced). The scalp lets no field into it, so hair is pushed off the head, never toward it. Below the grid's scale, each strand is also pushed from its neighbours' charge-weighted middle and by its own offset by ID, across itself and across the field, so a lock fans out into strands. Each strand bends from its root as a cantilever under those pushes times `strength` times its charge: each segment turns by the moment of the pushes beyond it, and the turns carry down the strand. Lengths are kept and the strand never kinks or goes into the scalp. About 45 ms for 100k strands of 16 points (`Budget.StaticEvaluation`). Evaluates in full (every strand reads the others). The GPU preview runs it while you drag or brush (its grid splatted and solved in compute: about 3 ms for 100k strands) | `strength` (mappable), `randomness`, `profile` |
| `Tangle` | Mats and wet hair: strands stick to a neighbor (roots within `radius`) over a span: onto it, or, when the two picked each other, both to the middle (at `strength` 1 they meet). Chain a few, after a tight Clump, for wet hair. Partners come from the core strands (those every preview holds), so the preview shows the render's tangles; with `sameClump` (on) from the strand's own clump, and after an Interpolate with a `regionMap` from its own region (`regionChannel`, `region` by default). A strand farther than `reach` from its partner there does not stick (0: no limit) | `fraction`, `strength` (both mappable), `radius`, `start`, `span`, `sameClump`, `regionChannel`, `reach` |
| `Wet` | Wet hair from one `wetness` slider (Bico et al., *Elastocapillary coalescence in wet hair*, Nature 2004): surface tension pulls wet strands into bundles against their stiffness, and a bundle forms only where the free length past where its strands join is long enough, longer for bigger bundles. So small clumps form near the root and merge into bigger and bigger ones toward the tip, each nested in one of the level above. `levels` sizes of clump, the biggest `clumpSize` apart, each level half the one above; level k (0 the smallest) engages from `start_k = sqrt(r_k / clumpSize) * lerp(0.9, 0.3, wetness)` of the strand on, blended in over 0.6 of that, at a tightness of `wetness (2 - wetness)` times 0.6 at the root to 0.92 at the tip. Inside, a chain of Clumps (largest first, each smaller one within the clumps of the one above), so each level's centers pull toward the next level's. Wetness 0 hands the strands on as they came: animate it from 1 to 0 to dry the hair. Writes the biggest clumps' numbers to `clumpChannel` (`clumpId`; the smaller levels to `clumpId1`, `clumpId2`...) and a `wetness` point channel for a shader. CPU only for now (the GPU preview runs up to it) | `wetness`, `clumpSize`, `levels`, `randomness`, `clumpChannel`, `seed` |
| `HairColor` | The hair's color per strand as one linear RGB (`colorR`, `colorG`, `colorB`; per point too with a tip color), a hair color in `aiStandardHair`'s base-color sense, which every host renders and exports as it is: Arnold's base color over no melanin (`hair_color` user data), the viewport's and the app's absorption by Chiang et al. 2016's mapping (`hairColorSigma`, the one Arnold maps a base color with), RenderMan's `rman_color`, Blender, Unreal's `groom_color`, USD `displayColor`, Alembic `Cd`, the cards bake. Modeled on RenderMan's PxrHairColor. `mode` `melanin` (natural, the default): the color whose absorption is the pigment's (`melanin` 0 white to 1 black, `redness` 0 brown to 1 red, both mappable; `hair_color.h` `melaninColor`, so it renders as `aiStandardHair`'s melanin would) dyed by `dye` (their absorptions added, as `aiStandardHair` combines melanin and base color); `mode` `color`: the `color` picked. Its `map` (a painted map by name, a color set on the scalp, or an image at the scalp's UVs) is read at the root: the color itself in mode color, a dye over the pigment in mode melanin. Per strand: `melaninRandom` (mode melanin, +/- melanin) or `valueRandom` (mode color, +/- share of brightness), then `hueRandom` (up to half a turn either way at 1) and `saturationRandom` (+/- share) in HSL with the lightness kept (grey and white stay neutral; `hair_color.h` `varyHairColor`); each draw mixes the strand's random number with its clump's by `clumpShare` (0 each strand its own, 1 the clump alike; the clump attribute `clumpChannel`, `clumpId` by default; a note when it reads nothing and is set off its default), keeping the spread the same. `greys` (mode melanin, mappable) is the share of strands with almost no pigment. It blends toward `tipColor` (varied with its strand) along the length by `tipStrength` x `tipProfile`, the start shifted per strand by up to `tipOffsetRandom` of the length (`tipStrength` mappable per point; above 0 the RGB is written per point too; at 0 an earlier color node's per-point colors are removed). It also writes each strand's `melanin` and `melaninRedness` as data (0 in mode color). It sets a look afresh: an earlier FeatherColor's iridescence is removed. The highlight (the Highlight section): `specular` (0-1; 0.3 is Arnold's full specular tint) and `roughness` (0-1), both mappable, each with a per-strand random spread (`specularRandom`, `roughnessRandom`), and `tilt` (cuticle tilt, degrees). Off their defaults they are written per strand (`specular`, `roughness`, `tilt`). The first HairColor making the output sets the highlight the viewport, the app, the cards and the Arnold, Maya and Blender shaders use: `create_hair_shader` sets the `aiStandardHair`'s specular tint to `min(1, specular / 0.3)`, its roughness to `0.1 + 0.4 x roughness`, its shift to the tilt, and reads the `specular` and `roughness` user data where they vary per strand. Bands and FeatherColor after it recolor its colors (see [Color patterns](looks-and-maps.md#color-patterns)). With several HairColor nodes, the look comes from the one making the output (walking up from the output, first inputs first; `groomlab.hair_color_node(groom)`, the graph stats' `hairColor`) | `mode`, `melanin`, `redness` (both mappable), `dye`, `color`, `map`, `greys` (mappable), `melaninRandom`, `valueRandom`, `hueRandom`, `saturationRandom`, `clumpShare`, `clumpChannel`, `tipColor`, `tipStrength` (mappable, per point), `tipProfile`, `tipOffsetRandom`, `specular`, `roughness` (both mappable), `specularRandom`, `roughnessRandom`, `tilt`, `seed` |
| `Bands` | Bands of another pigment along each hair, over the colors coming in (HairColor's, a tip too; else the pigment's): fur banded hair to hair (agouti, ticked, shaded). After a HairColor. `strength` (mappable, per point) mixes the band pigment (`melanin`, `redness`, `dye`, offset per strand by up to `randomness`) in where `profile` says, repeated `count` times root to tip; per strand the bands shift by up to `offsetRandom` of a period and stretch by up to `widthRandom`, so they do not line up across the coat. `guards` below 1 keeps that share of the strands as guard hairs (`guardsBy` `random` or the `longest`), which take the bands; the rest is undercoat, the incoming strand color times `undercoatDye` at every point (no bands, no tip color). Writes the same `colorR/G/B` channels (per strand and per point) every exporter reads; See [Color patterns](looks-and-maps.md#color-patterns) | `strength` (mappable, per point), `melanin`, `redness`, `dye`, `profile`, `count`, `guards`, `guardsBy`, `undercoatDye`, `randomness`, `offsetRandom`, `widthRandom`, `seed` |
| `FeatherColor` | Colors on feathers, over the colors coming in, after a HairColor (and a Propagate): a `pattern` in each feather's own flat space (vane space: `bars`, `chevrons`, `spots`, `fringe`, `eye`, a painted image of the feather with `texture`, or `none`) in its own pigment (`melanin`, `redness`, `dye`; an image its RGB) by `strength` on the `vanes` named, shaped by `count`, `width`, `angle`, `sharpness`, `offset` and `profile` and varied per feather (`offsetRandom`, `scaleRandom`, `angleRandom`); the down zone's barbs toward `downColor` by `downStrength`; and structural color, `iridescence` (mappable, per point; where: `iridescenceOn`) written as the per-point `iridescence` channel, its look `iridescenceColor` and `iridescenceShift`. Writes the `colorR/G/B` channels per point; the hosts' iridescence look is the first iridescent FeatherColor's making the output (`GroomSession::hairLook`). See [Color patterns](looks-and-maps.md#color-patterns) | `pattern`, `strength`, `melanin`, `redness`, `dye`, `vanes`, `count`, `width`, `angle`, `sharpness`, `offset`, `profile`, `texture`, `textureRight`, `offsetRandom`, `scaleRandom`, `angleRandom`, `downColor`, `downStrength`, `iridescence` (mappable, per point), `iridescenceColor`, `iridescenceShift`, `iridescenceOn`, `seed` |
| `RenderData` | Render controls per strand for the hair shader, beside the look (HairColor's color and highlight, which it leaves to it): `opacity` (mappable) times `opacityProfile` (a ramp root to tip), each strand up to `opacityRandom` less opaque, written per point (the `opacity` channel). The first RenderData making the output (walking up from it as for HairColor; the graph stats' `renderData`, `groomlab.render_data_node(groom)`): `create_hair_shader` wires opacity set off opaque into the `aiStandardHair`'s `opacity` (Arnold gets it varying, the curves made not opaque when any point is below 1); Blender's material mixes toward transparent by it. A later RenderData's values replace an earlier one's | `opacity` (mappable), `opacityProfile`, `opacityRandom`, `seed` |
| `Attribute` | Writes a per-strand channel `name` for later nodes or the shader: `value` is a number, a map read once, or an expression; a random value per strand is an expression of `rand` (`{"expression": "rand", "min": 0.2, "max": 0.8}`, or `mix(0.2, 0.8, rand)`) | `name`, `value` (mappable) |
| `Expression` (Fields) | A per-strand expression as a field for any exposed param: a block of lines with locals, reading the strand's shape (`$length`, `$longest`, `$reach`, `$straightness`, `$lift`, `$tipx/y/z`), root position, UV and normal, `$rand`, any strand channel (`$clumpId`, `$guideInfluence`, ...) and the node's own `variables` (numbers and vectors), remapped from 0-1 to `min`-`max`. E.g. `$length / $longest` to clump long strands harder. Per point with `$t` (arc length, root 0 to tip 1), `$pt`, `$px/py/pz`, `$pointIndex` or a point channel: e.g. `1 - $t` on Width. | `ToStrands` (Convert) | Each guide becomes a strand (the reverse of `ToGuides`): render accent guides as hair, run look nodes on guides, or `Merge` them with scattered strands. | `Twist` | Turns strands around an axis: the nearest curve of its `axes` input (a lock's guide, braid paths, ToGuides), or with nothing connected the middle of each clump (after Clump: the mean of its core strands, the ones every preview holds; a clump without one does not turn), for ropes and twisted locks | `turns` (mappable, per unit length; negative turns the other way), `profile`, `clumpChannel` |
| `Braid` | Braids strands along its `braids` paths (drawn curves, guides), or with nothing on `braids` along paths it picks among the strands, about `region` apart (box braids): `three`, `fishtail`, `rope` or `cornrow`, gathering the strands rooted within `region` of a path's root (large: a ponytail), or with `feed` picked up within `feedDistance` of the path along its first `feedUntil`; `alongScalp` sweeps far-off hair over the scalp into the braid. Writes `braidId` and `braidFlat` channels | `style`, `sections`, `dutch`, `depth`, `phase`, `crossingsProfile` (ramp), `sectionTwist`, `resolution`, `maxSegments`, `width`, `widthProfile` (ramp), `crossings`, `lobeFill`, `region`, `feed`, `feedDistance`, `feedUntil`, `feedGrowth`, `endAtPath`, `alongScalp`, `gather`, `tail`, `strays`, `fuzz`, `seed`, `display` |
| `Subdivide` | Smooth strands from few points: each level doubles every strand's segments along a smooth curve through its points (centripetal Catmull-Rom: it passes through them, no loops or overshoot), so a curl grown on few points comes out round; widths and per-point channels follow linearly, strands of fewer than 3 points stay. Unlike `Resample` it asks the Scatters before it for nothing: the nodes before it work on the few points, and only what comes after it pays for the many, so put it last. `levels` for renders and exports (default 2: 4 times the segments), `viewportLevels` for the viewport (default 1; at 0 it hands the strands on and the GPU preview runs the nodes before it). CPU only | `levels`, `viewportLevels` |
| `Smooth` | Takes out each strand's bumps up to `scale` of its length (after noise, interpolation or sculpting), keeping lengths and roots: its direction along its length is averaged over a window that wide by arc length (three box filters, as a Gaussian), so a strand smooths the same at any point count and any scene size; `scale` 1 straightens it along the line from its root to its tip. `strength` blends each point toward that shape (with `profile` along the length), then the segment lengths are put back from the root. The default (0.09) takes out about what 4 passes of the old neighbour average did on 16-point strands. Strands or guides | `strength` (mappable), `scale`, `profile` |
| `Direction` | Points each strand (turned about its root, shape kept) by its `inclination` from the scalp's normal (0 straight out, 90 flat along the scalp) and its `roll` around the normal from where roll 0 points, `along` the scalp's U direction (`tangent`, from its UVs; without them `direction`) or a world `direction` laid onto the scalp, with random spreads per strand (by ID); `strength` turns part of the way. Yeti's Direction. Strands not rooted on the scalp stay | `inclination`, `roll`, `strength` (all mappable), `along`, `direction`, `inclinationRandom`, `rollRandom`, `seed` |
| `Bend` | Bends strands about their roots toward a direction (`along` the scalp's `normal` at the root, its U `tangent`, or a world `direction` laid onto the scalp at the root, as a comb lays hair; a strand off the scalp takes it as it is): the strand's direction at t turns by `angle` x the share of [`start`, `end`] behind t, so the bend is spread evenly between the two and the strand holds its shape past the end; lengths kept. A negative angle bends away (with the normal: down onto the scalp); a strand standing straight up bends over along the scalp's U. A strand never turns (root to tip) past the direction it bends toward, nor past its opposite bending away, and along the normal never below the scalp's tangent plane: `angle` 180 with `start` and `end` 0 points every strand along the direction (combing scales down a body), along the normal it turns each strand whole about its root (volume at the roots; negative: down onto the scalp). `direction` takes a vector expression per strand. `randomness` scales each strand's angle down by up to that share. Yeti's Bend | `angle` (mappable), `start`, `end`, `along`, `direction`, `randomness`, `seed` |
| `Transform` | Moves, turns and scales strands: each about its own root in its root's frame (`space` `strand`: x along the scalp's U, y its normal, z across), or the whole set about `pivot` in world axes (`world`); `translate`, `rotate` (degrees, x then y then z), `scale` (mappable), each with a random spread per strand (by ID); the common `affect` blends each strand toward its transformed self.
